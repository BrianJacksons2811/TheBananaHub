FAQ

Common questions clients ask about maintenance.

Do you maintain non-WordPress sites?

Yes, depending on the platform. We’ll confirm after a quick assessment.

What if my site gets hacked?

Standard and Pro include stronger security. Recovery work may be quoted based on severity.

Can I request changes when I need them?

Yes. Basic plan is ad hoc; Standard and Pro include included edits/support.

Is hosting included?

No. Hosting is billed separately. We can recommend the right hosting for your site.
